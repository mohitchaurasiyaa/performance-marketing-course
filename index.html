    Shuruaat: Performance Marketing hai kya?

    Performance marketing matlab aisi marketing jahan aap result ke hisaab se paisa lagate ho aur result ke hisaab se hi judge hote ho. Har rupaye ka hisaab hota hai: kitna kharch hua, kitne leads aaye, kitni sales hui, kitna profit bana. Brand marketing "log hume yaad rakhein" pe focus karti hai; performance marketing "aaj kitne log khareedenge aur kis cost pe" pe.

    0.1Performance marketer ka actual kaam

    Log sochte hain ki kaam sirf "ads chalana" hai. Asal mein ek achha performance marketer ye 6 kaam karta hai:

    1. Business samajhna

    Product ka margin, AOV, repeat rate, ek customer kitne mein afford ho sakta hai.

    2. Tracking set karna

    Pixel, CAPI, GA4, GTM. Data galat hai to har decision galat hoga.

    3. Campaign banana

    Sahi objective, structure, budget aur bidding Meta/Google pe.

    4. Creative strategy

    Kaunsa message, hook, offer aur format kis audience ko jayega.

    5. Optimize karna

    Numbers padh ke decide karna: kya band karein, kya scale karein, kya test karein.

    6. Report aur explain

    Client/boss ko simple bhasha mein batana ki paisa kahan gaya aur aage kya plan hai.

    0.22026 mein game kaise badla hai

    Pichhle 2-3 saal mein platforms ne bahut kuch automate kar diya hai. Ye samajhna zaroori hai warna aap purane tutorials se purani strategy seekh loge:

    • Targeting AI ke paas chali gayi hai. Meta ka Andromeda system aur Advantage+ ab khud decide karte hain ki ad kise dikhana hai. Interest-stacking jaisi cheezein ab utna kaam nahi karti.
    • Creative hi naya targeting hai. Ad mein kya bola, kaise dikhaya, kis problem ko pakda, isi se algorithm samajhta hai ki ad kis type ke logon ka hai.

    • Privacy rules strict hain. Consent Mode, cookie restrictions, India mein DPDP Act. Tracking ab "install karo aur bhool jao" nahi raha.

    1.1Customer Journey & Marketing Funnel

    Koi bhi insaan pehli baar aapka ad dekh ke turant nahi khareedta (sasta impulse product chhod ke). Wo kuch stages se guzarta hai. In stages ko funnel kehte hain kyunki har stage pe log kam hote jaate hain.

    TOFU · Awareness — "Mujhe ye problem hai / ye cheez exist karti hai"
    MOFU · Consideration — "Kaunsa option best hai? Reviews kya kehte hain?"
    BOFU · Conversion — "Abhi khareedta hoon / form bharta hoon"
    Retention — "Phir se khareedunga"
    Advocacy — "Doston ko bataunga"

    Awareness ke 5 levels (Eugene Schwartz ka model)

    Ye ek purana copywriting framework hai jo aaj bhi ad likhne mein bahut kaam aata hai. Customer jis level pe hai, ad ki opening wahin se karni padti hai:

    1. Unaware — Problem ka bhi pata nahi. Ad ko story ya relatable situation se kholo. ("Roz subah baal jhadte hain aur aap sochte ho weather hai?")
    2. Problem aware — Problem pata hai, solution nahi. Problem ko naam do aur empathy dikhao.
    3. Solution aware — Solutions pata hain, aapka brand nahi. Batao aapka solution alag/better kyun hai.
    4. Product aware — Aapko jaanta hai, convince nahi hua. Proof, reviews, demo, guarantee.
    5. Most aware — Khareedne ko ready. Seedha offer: "Aaj 20% off, free delivery."
    Practical rule

    Cold audience (jisne aapko kabhi nahi dekha) ko "Buy now 20% off" dikhana sabse common galti hai. Unke liye problem/story wala ad. Retargeting audience (jo site visit kar chuke) ke liye offer wala ad.

    Funnel ka modern sach: ye seedhi line nahi hai

    Google isko "messy middle" kehta hai. Customer Instagram reel dekhta hai, phir Google pe search karta hai, YouTube review dekhta hai, Amazon pe price compare karta hai, WhatsApp pe dost se poochta hai, phir 5 din baad direct website pe aake khareedta hai. Isliye:

    • Ek hi platform ko poora credit dena galat hai (attribution Module 4 mein detail mein).
    • Meta aur Google ek dusre ke competitor nahi, partner hain. Meta demand create karta hai, Google Search demand capture karta hai.
    • Har touchpoint pe brand ka message consistent hona chahiye.

    Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

    Customer product nahi, progress khareedta hai. Drill machine nahi, deewar mein ched chahiye. Ched bhi nahi, family photo deewar pe chahiye. Ad likhne se pehle ye 3 sawaal poocho:

    1. Functional job: Practically kya kaam hona chahiye? (Paani soft ho jaye)
    2. Emotional job: Kaisa feel karna chahta hai? (Baal aur skin ki tension khatam)
    3. Social job: Dusre use kaise dekhein? (Smart, family ka khayal rakhne wala)

    Indian market ki khaas psychology

    • Value-seeking: "Paisa vasool" feeling zaroori. Combo offers, free gift, "₹X per day" framing kaam karti hai.
    • Trust deficit online: Naye brand pe bharosa kam. COD option, real customer videos, physical address, GST number, WhatsApp support conversion badhate hain.
    • Family decision: Bade purchase (education, home, car) mein family involved. Ad mein family angle aur "ghar walon ko dikhao" type share-friendly content.
    • Festive calendar: Diwali, Navratri, wedding season, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, end-of-season sale. CPM inn dino badhta hai, par buying intent bhi.
    • Language: Tier 2/3 cities mein Hinglish ya regional language ke ads aksar English se better perform karte hain. Test karo.

    1.3Revenue vs Profit

    Ye confusion bahut business barbaad karta hai. Revenue = jo paisa aaya (sales). Profit = sab kharche nikaal ke jo bacha. ₹10 lakh ki sale ho sakti hai aur fir bhi loss ho sakta hai.

    Profit ki seedhi Revenue (sale)
    − COGS (product banane/khareedne ka cost)
    = Gross Profit
    − Shipping, packaging, payment gateway fee, COD charges, returns/RTO loss
    = Contribution (ad se pehle)
    − Ad spend
    = Contribution after marketing
    − Fixed costs (rent, salary, software)
    = Net Profit
    Example: ek skincare D2C brand ka mahina
    Item₹
    Revenue (1,000 orders × ₹1,500)15,00,000
    COGS (30%)− 4,50,000
    Gross profit10,50,000
    Shipping + packaging (₹120/order)− 1,20,000
    Payment fee + COD + RTO loss (₹130/order)− 1,30,000
    Contribution before ads8,00,000
    Ad spend− 6,00,000
    Contribution after ads2,00,000
    Fixed costs− 2,50,000
    Net profit− 50,000

    Ad manager mein ROAS = 15,00,000 / 6,00,000 = 2.5x dikhega aur sab khush honge. Par business loss mein hai. Isliye performance marketer ko poori seedhi pata honi chahiye.

    1.4CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost

    Ek naya customer laane mein kitna kharcha hua.

    FormulaCAC = Total marketing + sales cost ÷ Naye customers ki sankhya

    Example: Mahine mein ₹3,00,000 ads pe, ₹50,000 agency fee, 500 naye customers → CAC = 3,50,000 / 500 = ₹700.

    • CPA vs CAC: CPA (cost per acquisition) platform ka number hai, sirf ad spend ÷ conversions. CAC business ka number hai, saare acquisition kharche ÷ sirf naye customers. CAC hamesha CPA se zyada ya barabar hota hai.
    • Blended CAC = total marketing spend ÷ total naye customers (organic waale bhi). Paid CAC = sirf paid spend ÷ paid se aaye naye customers. Dono track karo.

    1.5LTV — Lifetime Value

    Ek customer poori zindagi (relationship) mein aapko kitna value deta hai. Ye batata hai ki aap ek customer laane pe maximum kitna kharch kar sakte ho.

    Simple LTV (revenue)LTV = AOV × Saal mein kitni baar khareeda × Kitne saal customer raha
    Sahi LTV (margin based, isi ko use karo)LTV = AOV × Purchase frequency × Lifespan × Contribution margin %

    Example: AOV ₹1,500, saal mein 3 baar, 2 saal tak, contribution margin 53% → LTV = 1,500 × 3 × 2 × 0.53 = ₹4,770.

    Dhyan rakho
    • Naye business ke paas 2 saal ka data nahi hota. Tab 90-day ya 180-day LTV use karo (pehle 90 din mein ek customer ne average kitna kharch kiya).
    • Services (coaching, real estate, construction) mein aksar ek hi sale hoti hai. Wahan LTV ≈ ek deal ka margin + referral value.
    • LTV future ka andaza hai, guarantee nahi. Isse conservatively lo.

    1.6AOV — Average Order Value

    FormulaAOV = Total revenue ÷ Total orders

    AOV badhana sabse aasaan profit lever hai, kyunki ek order laane ka ad cost same rehta hai, par order bada ho jata hai.

    AOV badhane ke tareeke

    • Bundles/combos: "Shampoo + conditioner + serum kit" — single product se 30-40% zyada AOV.
    • Free shipping threshold: Current AOV ₹800 hai to free shipping ₹999 pe rakho.
    • Quantity discount: "2 lo 10% off, 3 lo 20% off".
    • Upsell/cross-sell: Cart ya checkout pe "Isse saath mein log ye bhi lete hain".
    • Free gift above X: "₹1,499 se upar free travel pouch".

    1.7Gross Margin & Contribution Margin

    Gross margin %(Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100
    Contribution margin % (ads se pehle)(Revenue − COGS − saare variable costs per order) ÷ Revenue × 100
    Variable costs = shipping, packaging, payment gateway, COD fee, RTO/return loss, marketplace commission

    Upar wale example mein: gross margin = 70%, contribution margin = 8,00,000 / 15,00,000 = 53.3%.

    Kyun zaroori hai

    Contribution margin hi batata hai ki ads ke liye kitni "jagah" hai. 70% margin waale product (skincare, digital course) ke liye ads aasaan hain. 15% margin waale product (electronics reselling) pe paid ads se profit nikalna bahut mushkil hai. Client lene se pehle hi ye check karo.

    1.8LTV : CAC Ratio

    FormulaLTV : CAC = LTV (margin-based) ÷ CAC
    RatioMatlabAction
    < 1 : 1Har customer pe lossTurant fix: offer, pricing, CVR, ya targeting
    1 : 1 – 2 : 1Survive kar rahe ho, grow nahiRetention aur AOV pe kaam
    ~3 : 1Healthy (industry ka common benchmark)Dheere-dheere scale
    > 5 : 1Shayad kam spend kar rahe hoAggressively scale karo, market share le lo

    Example: LTV ₹4,770, CAC ₹700 → ratio ≈ 6.8 : 1. Iska matlab business zyada aggressive ho sakta hai.

    1.9Break-even ROAS

    Wo ROAS jis pe na profit na loss (sirf us order ke level pe). Iske neeche har sale pe paisa doob raha hai.

    FormulaBreak-even ROAS = 1 ÷ Contribution margin % (decimal mein)
    Break-even CPA = AOV × Contribution margin %

    Example: Contribution margin 53.3% → BE ROAS = 1 / 0.533 = 1.88x. Break-even CPA = 1,500 × 0.533 = ₹800. Matlab ek order pe ₹800 tak ad pe kharch karo to zero profit; usse kam to profit.

    Contribution marginBreak-even ROAS
    80%1.25x
    60%1.67x
    50%2.00x
    40%2.50x
    30%3.33x
    20%5.00x
    Target ROAS kaise set karein

    Target ROAS = break-even ROAS se upar, jitna profit chahiye. Agar har order pe 15% net profit chahiye aur contribution margin 53% hai, to ad ke liye 53 − 15 = 38% bachta hai → target ROAS = 1 / 0.38 = 2.63x. Agar business repeat customers pe chalta hai (high LTV), to first order pe break-even ya thoda loss bhi accept ho sakta hai.

    1.10Payback Period

    CAC ka paisa wapas aane mein kitne mahine lagte hain. Cash flow ke liye bahut zaroori, khaas karke chhote business ke liye jinke paas paisa limited hai.

    FormulaPayback (mahine) = CAC ÷ Ek customer se har mahine milne wala contribution

    Example: CAC ₹700. Customer har 4 mahine mein ₹1,500 ka order karta hai (53% margin) → monthly contribution = 1,500 × 0.53 / 4 ≈ ₹199 → payback ≈ 3.5 mahine. Pehle order se hi ₹800 contribution aata hai jo CAC se zyada hai, to asal mein payback pehle order pe hi ho gaya. Subscription/SaaS mein ye calculation sabse zyada use hoti hai. 12 mahine se kam payback ko generally healthy maana jata hai.

    1.11Ad Metrics: CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, CPL

    MetricFormulaKya batata hai
    ImpressionsAd kitni baar dikhaReach ≠ impressions. Reach = unique log, impressions = total views
    FrequencyImpressions ÷ ReachEk insaan ne average kitni baar dekha. Cold audience pe 3-4 se upar jaaye to fatigue aa sakti hai
    CPMSpend ÷ Impressions × 1,0001,000 baar ad dikhane ka cost. Audience ki competition aur ad quality batata hai
    CTRClicks ÷ Impressions × 100Ad kitna interesting hai. Meta pe "Link CTR" dekho, "All CTR" nahi
    CPCSpend ÷ ClicksEk click ka cost. CPM aur CTR dono pe depend
    CVRConversions ÷ Clicks × 100Landing page + offer kitna strong hai
    CPLSpend ÷ LeadsLead gen business (real estate, education, services) ka main metric
    CPASpend ÷ ConversionsEk desired action (purchase, signup) ka cost

    Sabse powerful equation: sab metrics kaise jude hain

    Funnel mathCPC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR)
    CPA = CPC ÷ CVR = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR × CVR)

    Example: CPM ₹150, CTR 1.2%, CVR 2% → CPC = 150 / 12 = ₹12.5 → CPA = 12.5 / 0.02 = ₹625.

    Ab dekho: agar CTR 1.2% se 1.8% ho jaye (better hook), CPA ₹417 ho jayega. Agar CVR 2% se 3% ho jaye (better landing page), CPA bhi ₹417. CPA kam karne ke teen hi lever hain: CPM, CTR, CVR. Har optimization in teeno mein se kisi ek ko hilati hai.

    1.12Conversion Rate & ROAS (aur MER)

    ROASROAS = Ad se aaya revenue ÷ Ad spend
    MER / Blended ROAS (business level)MER = Total revenue (sab channels) ÷ Total marketing spend
    • Platform ROAS pe aankh band karke bharosa mat karo. Meta aur Google dono ek hi sale ka credit le sakte hain (double counting). Isliye business level pe MER dekho.
    • ROAS ≠ profit. 4x ROAS bhi loss ho sakta hai agar margin 20% hai. Hamesha break-even ROAS se compare karo.
    • Conversion rate do tarah: ad level (conversions ÷ clicks) aur site level (orders ÷ sessions). Indian e-commerce mein site CVR aksar 1–3% ke beech rehta hai; category ke hisaab se kaafi badalta hai.

    Incrementality: asli sawaal

    "Kya ye sale ad ki wajah se hui, ya ye insaan waise bhi khareedta?" Retargeting aur brand search campaigns aksar high ROAS dikhate hain kyunki wo already-ready logon ko pakadte hain. Advanced teams isse lift tests / holdout tests se measure karti hain (ek group ko ads dikhao, dusre ko nahi, farak dekho). Meta aur Google dono mein conversion lift study ke options hain. Beginner level pe itna yaad rakho: jitna lower funnel, utna kam incremental.

    2.6Placements

    Ad kahan dikhega: Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore, Messenger, Marketplace, Audience Network, Threads, search results, etc.

    Budget kahan set karein

    TypeKaise kaam karta haiKab use

    Kitna budget chahiye? (minimum logic)

    Rule of thumbDaily budget per ad set ≥ Target CPA × 50 ÷ 7
    Example: Target CPA ₹500 → 500 × 50 / 7 ≈ ₹3,570/din learning phase jaldi exit karne ke liye

    Itna budget nahi hai? Koi baat nahi. Options: (1) kam ad sets rakho taaki saara data ek jagah aaye, (2) higher-funnel event pe optimize karo (Add to Cart, Initiate Checkout, Lead) jo zyada baar hota hai, (3) "Learning limited" status accept karo; campaign phir bhi results de sakta hai.

    Learning phase

    • Naya ad set ya bada edit hone pe Meta "learning" mein jata hai. Is dauran results unstable hote hain.
    • Learning reset hoti hai jab: creative add/edit, targeting change, optimization event change, budget mein bada change (roughly 20-30% se zyada ek baar mein), bid strategy change, 7+ din pause.
    • Rule: edits ko batch karo aur har 2-3 din mein ek baar karo, roz nahi. Naye campaign ko kam se kam 3-7 din do judge karne se pehle.

    Bid strategies

    StrategyMatlabKab
    Highest volume (lowest cost)Budget mein maximum resultsDefault, starting point
    Cost per result goal (cost cap)Average CPA is target ke aas paas rakhoJab CPA control zaroori ho; target bahut tight rakhoge to spend nahi hoga
    ROAS goal (min ROAS)Har sale pe minimum ROASE-commerce with purchase value data
    Bid capHar auction mein max bidAdvanced; experienced buyers only
    Highest valueZyada value wale purchases dhoondhnaJab basket size mein bahut variation ho

    Scaling: winning campaign ko bada kaise karein

    1. Vertical scaling: Budget har 2-3 din mein ~20% badhao. Ek baar mein double karoge to performance aksar girti hai.
    2. Horizontal scaling: Naye creatives/angles add karo, nayi geography, naya campaign with proven creatives.
    3. Creative scaling (2026 mein sabse important): Winning ad ke angle pe 5 naye variations (alag hook, alag creator, alag format). Andromeda ko naye "entry points" milte hain naye log tak pahunchne ke liye.
    4. Scale karte waqt CPA thoda badhega hi (diminishing returns). Dekho ki marginal CPA break-even se neeche hai ya nahi.

    2.10Custom Audiences & Lookalikes

    Custom audience ke sources

    • Website (pixel): all visitors, specific URL visitors, event-based (AddToCart), time spent. Maximum 180 din.
    • Customer list: phone/email upload (Meta isse hash karke match karta hai). Phone numbers country code ke saath (+91) do.
    • Engagement: video viewers, Instagram profile engagers, page engagers, lead form openers, WhatsApp messagers.
    • App activity, offline activity, catalog.

    Lookalike audience

    Meta aapke seed audience jaise naye log dhoondhta hai. 1% = sabse similar (India mein bhi bahut badi audience, lakhs mein), 10% = broad.

    • Seed quality sabse important: top customers (high LTV) ka lookalike, saare customers se better.
    • Seed mein kam se kam 100 log chahiye; 1,000-5,000 ideal.
    • Andromeda era mein lookalikes ki power kam hui hai kyunki broad targeting already ye kaam karti hai. Inhe "signal" ki tarah use karo ya test karo, ye guaranteed winner nahi hain.
    Privacy

    Customer list sirf unki upload karo jinhone aapko data diya aur marketing ke liye consent hai. India ka DPDP Act (Digital Personal Data Protection) personal data ke use pe rules lagata hai. Kharidi hui lists kabhi upload mat karo; Meta ki policy ke bhi khilaaf hai.

    Video ad metrics

    • Hook rate = 3-second video plays ÷ impressions. Kitne log ruk gaye. 25-30%+ ko achha maana jata hai.
    • Hold rate = ThruPlays (15 sec ya poora) ÷ 3-second plays. Kitne log dekhte rahe.
    • Hook rate low → pehle 3 second badlo. Hold rate low → beech ka content boring hai.
    Kill rule (example)

    Agar kisi ad ne target CPA ka 2-3x kharch kar diya aur ek bhi conversion nahi, to pause. Agar CTR ok hai par conversions nahi, to pehle landing page check karo, ad ko blame mat karo.

    3.3Keyword Research

    Process

    1. Seed list banao: Product/service ke naam, problems, use-cases, location ke saath. Client se poocho: "customer phone pe pehla sawaal kya poochte hain?"
    2. Google Keyword Planner (Tools → Planning): seed daalo, location India/city chuno. Volume, competition aur top-of-page bid range dekho.
    3. Google autocomplete, "People also ask", related searches — free aur real searches.
    4. Search Console (agar website hai): kaunse queries pe already traffic aa raha hai.
    5. Competitor sites aur unke ads dekho (Google Ads Transparency Center).
    6. Group karo theme + intent ke hisaab se, aur har group ke liye ek landing page decide karo.

    Keyword ko evaluate kaise karein

    • Intent > volume. "water softener price bhopal" (50 searches) > "what is hard water" (5,000 searches) ek seller ke liye.
    • Modifiers jo buying intent dikhate hain: price, cost, buy, near me, best, vs, review, dealer, installation, service, book, online.
    • Modifiers jo intent kam karte hain: free, kya hai, meaning, DIY, jobs, course, PDF, download, salary.
    • Long-tail keywords (3-5 words) kam volume par zyada specific, zyada CVR.
    • Max CPC affordability: Break-even CPC = Break-even CPA × expected CVR. Agar BE CPA ₹800 aur CVR 4% → ₹32 tak ka CPC afford hai.

    AI Max for Search (2026)

    AI Max ek feature suite hai jo aapke existing Search campaign ke upar lagta hai. Ye alag campaign type nahi hai (PMax ki tarah). Isme teen cheezein hain:

    • Search term matching: aapke keywords aur website ke basis pe naye relevant queries dhoondhta hai (broad match + "keywordless" logic).
    • Text customization: search ke hisaab se headlines/descriptions automatically adjust karta hai.
    • Final URL expansion: user ko website ke sabse relevant page pe bhejta hai, sirf aapke diye URL pe nahi.
    2026 timeline
    • AI Max for Search 2026 mein beta se bahar aa gaya aur naye Search campaigns ke liye default setting ban raha hai.
    • Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) ko AI Max replace karega. Automatic migration pehle September 2026 plan thi, ab February 2027 tak shift ho gayi hai. Automatically created assets aur campaign-level broad match setting ka upgrade September 2026 se chal raha hai.
    • Google Search ke AI Overviews aur AI Mode mein ads ke liye eligible setups: AI Max ya broad match wale Search campaigns, Shopping, aur PMax. Sirf exact/phrase match wale Search campaigns (bina AI Max) wahan eligible nahi hain.
    AI Max use karne ka safe tarika
    1. Pehle conversion tracking solid karo. AI ka khana data hai.
    2. Ek campaign pe experiment ke roop mein on karo, sab pe ek saath nahi.
    3. Brand controls (brand inclusions/exclusions), URL exclusions (careers, blog, privacy policy pages), aur negative keywords set karo.
    4. Har 2-3 din search terms report padho. AI wide jaal phenkta hai; irrelevant queries ko negative karo.
    5. 2-4 hafte baad CPA/ROAS compare karo bina AI Max wale period se.

    Note: aaj ke Google mein exact match bhi "close variants" (plural, spelling, same intent) pe chalta hai. Match types ab "kitna strict" ke bajaye "kitna flexible" ki scale hain.

    Practical approach (2026)

    Chhota budget aur naya account: phrase + exact, manual control. Jab 30+ conversions/month aa jayein: high-performing ad groups mein broad match + smart bidding test karo. Ek hi keyword ko teen match types mein daal ke ek hi ad group mein mat bharo; ab iski zarurat nahi.

    3.8Quality Score, Ad Rank aur Bidding

    Quality Score (1–10, keyword level)

    • Expected CTR: Is keyword pe aapka ad kitna click hoga (competitors ke comparison mein).
    • Ad relevance: Ad keyword/search se kitna match karta hai.
    • Landing page experience: Page relevant, fast, mobile-friendly, trustworthy hai ya nahi.

    High QS = kam CPC aur better position. QS 7+ achha hai. Ad Rank (position) bid, quality, assets, search context aur Ad Rank thresholds se decide hota hai. Matlab achha ad kam bid pe bhi upar aa sakta hai.

    GA4 se linked Google Ads account mein advertising data (cookies, identifiers) jayega ya nahi, iska ek hi control ab Consent Mode hai. Pehle Google Signals toggle bhi isme role play karta tha; ab Google Signals sirf signed-in users ke data se GA4 reports enrich karne tak limited hai. Isliye 2024 ke purane consent guides aadhe outdated hain — setup audit karo.

    India: DPDP Act

    Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 India ka data privacy law hai, jiske rules phase-wise implement ho rahe hain. Marketer ke liye practical matlab:

    • Personal data (naam, phone, email) collect karne se pehle clear notice aur consent.
    • Sirf utna data lo jitna zaroori hai, aur jis purpose ke liye liya usi ke liye use karo.
    • User ko data delete/withdraw karne ka option.
    • Lead forms pe privacy policy link aur consent checkbox/line.
    • Exact legal requirements ke liye client ko legal advisor se confirm karwane bolo. Aap marketer ho, lawyer nahi.

    4.9Server-side tracking (advanced, par future yahi hai)

    Server-side GTM (sGTM): browser saara data pehle aapke apne server (e.g. track.yoursite.com) pe bhejta hai, wahan se server Meta CAPI, GA4, Google Ads ko bhejta hai.

    • Fayde: ad blockers ka asar kam, first-party cookies zyada time tak, page speed better (kam scripts browser mein), data pe control (kya bhejna kya nahi).
    • Cost: Server hosting (Google Cloud, ya Stape jaise managed providers). Small business ke liye monthly ek chhota kharch.
    • Kab chahiye: Monthly ad spend ₹2-3 lakh+ ho, ya tracking loss noticeable ho. Beginners pehle client-side + platform CAPI integrations se shuru karein.

    5.1Creative Strategy ki shuruaat: customer research

    Achha ad "creativity" se nahi, customer ki apni language se banta hai. Ad likhne se pehle ye research karo:

    5.2Hooks

    Hook = ad ke pehle 1-3 second (video) ya pehli line/visual (static). Agar hook fail, to baaki ad koi nahi dekhega. Feed mein aapke ad ka competition doosre ads se nahi, dost ki reel aur memes se hai.

    Hook typeExample
    Call-out (audience ko pukaaro)"Bhopal mein ghar hai aur taps pe safed daag aate hain? Ye dekho."
    Problem statement"Har mahine ₹800 ka shampoo, phir bhi baal jhad rahe hain?"
    Curiosity gap"Mera plumber ne ek cheez batayi jo kisi company ne nahi batayi..."
    Bold claim / result"14 din, 0 scaling. Ye hamari bathroom ki before-after hai."
    Contrarian"RO lagwana band karo. Pehle ye test karo."
    Mistake"Ye 3 galtiyan 90% parents JEE coaching chunte waqt karte hain."
    Story open"Pichhle saal Diwali pe humare saath kuch aisa hua..."

    Social proof"2,000 Bhopal families ne ye switch kiya. Kyun?"
    Question"Aapka paani hard hai ya soft? 10 second mein pata karo."
    Hook = 3 layers

    Video hook mein teen cheezein saath kaam karti hain: visual (pehla frame), text overlay (sound off mein bhi samajh aaye), spoken line. Teeno ek hi message ko support karein. Ek hi video ke 3-5 alag hooks bana ke test karna sabse sasta creative test hai.

    5.3Pain Points & Desires

    Pain point = jo dard customer abhi feel kar raha hai. Desire = jo future wo chahta hai. Ad dono ke beech ka bridge dikhata hai: "Abhi ye problem hai → hamare saath ye life".

    Pain/desire ki depth (surface se deep tak)

    5.4Offers

    Offer sirf discount nahi hai. Offer = poora deal jo customer ko milta hai: product + price + bonus + guarantee + urgency.

    5.5Ad Angles

    Angle = kis "nazariye" se product ko pitch kar rahe ho. Ek hi product ke 10 angles ho sakte hain, aur har angle alag type ke logon ko pakadta hai. Andromeda era mein angle diversity hi targeting hai.

    Problem-solution

    Problem dikhao, product solution.

    Us vs them

    Alternatives se comparison (bina competitor ka naam liye).

    Cost of inaction

    "Kuch na karne ka nuksan" — money, health, time.

    Social proof

    Reviews, numbers, testimonials compilation.

    Founder story

    Brand kyun banaya, kya alag hai.

    Education / myth-busting

    "Log sochte hain X, sach Y hai".

    Persona-specific

    Working women, students, new parents, small shop owners.

    Occasion

    Diwali gifting, wedding season, exam time, monsoon.

    Behind the scenes

    Kaise banta hai, quality check, factory.

    Objection handling

    "Mehenga hai?" → per day cost; "Kaam karega?" → guarantee.

    Creative matrix (systematic tareeka)

    Ek table banao: rows mein angles (5-6), columns mein personas (2-3) aur formats (UGC video, static, carousel). Har cell ek ad idea hai. Isse random creatives ki jagah planned diversity milti hai.

    5.6UGC (User Generated Content)

    UGC = aam logon jaisa dikhne wala content (phone se shoot, real feel). Ye ads jaisa nahi lagta isliye log rukte hain aur trust karte hain.

    UGC formats

    • Testimonial: "Maine 2 mahine use kiya, ye hua."
    • Unboxing / first impression
    • Problem-solution demo: before → use → after
    • "3 reasons why I switched" listicle
    • Talking head + B-roll
    • Street interview / reaction
    • Green screen (creator review screenshots ke saamne)

    UGC creators kaise laayein

    • Existing happy customers se (free product/discount ke badle).
    • Instagram pe micro-creators (5k-50k followers) jinki audience aapki category ki hai.
    • UGC platforms/marketplaces aur freelance creators.
    • Usage rights likhit mein lo: kitne time tak, kaunse platforms pe ads mein use kar sakte ho.
    • Partnership ads (Meta): creator ke handle se ad chalao. Inka CTR aur trust aksar brand handle se better hota hai.

    Creator brief template

    • Product & ek line mein kya karta hai
    • Target persona (kisse baat kar rahe ho)
    • Angle + 3 hook options (creator apne words mein bole)
    • Must-say points (3 max) aur don'ts (medical claims nahi, competitor naam nahi)
    • Shots list: face close-up, product in hand, usage, result
    • Format: 9:16, 20-45 sec, natural light, raw footage bhi bhejein
    • CTA

    5.7Static Creatives

    • Brand logo chhota, product clear.
    • 4:5 feed ke liye, 9:16 stories/reels ke liye (safe zones ka dhyan).
    • Thumb-stop colors: feed ke background se contrast.

    5.9Creative Testing System

    1. Hypothesis likho: "Cost-of-inaction angle, problem-aware audience ke liye, social proof angle se kam CPA dega kyunki..."
    2. Ek variable test karo: Angle test (alag messages), ya hook test (same body, alag openings), ya format test.
    3. Testing campaign (ABO ya Advantage+ with new ads), har concept ko barabar mauka.
    4. Kaafi data: har ad pe kam se kam 1-2x target CPA spend, ya kuch hazaar impressions, judge karne se pehle.
    5. Metrics ladder: Hook rate → CTR → CPC → CVR → CPA/ROAS. Early signals (hook rate, CTR) se losers jaldi pakdo.
    6. Winner ko scaling campaign mein move karo (post ID se taaki social proof/likes saath rahein).
    7. Learning log maintain karo: date, concept, angle, result, insight. 3 mahine baad ye aapki sabse valuable file hogi.
    Kitne creatives chahiye?

    Rough guide: chhote accounts (₹1-3 lakh/month) — 4-8 naye creatives per month. Medium (₹5-20 lakh) — 10-20. Bade accounts — 30+. Creative fatigue ka sign: frequency badh rahi, CTR gir raha, CPA badh raha same audience pe.

    5.10CRO: Landing Page Optimization

    Ad customer ko darwaze tak laata hai; landing page usse andar bulata hai. CVR 2% se 3% hone ka matlab hai CPA 33% kam — bina ad spend badhaye.

    Headline

    • Ad ka promise turant repeat ho (message match).
    • Outcome bolo, feature nahi: "Hard water se hair fall band" > "Advanced ion-exchange technology".
    • Subheadline mein "kaise" aur "kisko".
    • 5-second test: kisi ko page 5 sec dikhao, poocho kya bechte ho aur kisko. Nahi bata paya to headline fail.

    Offer

    • Offer above the fold dikhe: price, discount, free gift, guarantee.
    • Price ke saath value justify karo: per-day cost, comparison, kya-kya milega list.
    • Shipping cost aur delivery time pehle hi batao (checkout pe surprise = cart abandonment).

    CTA

    • Action + benefit: "Free Demo Book Karein" > "Submit".
    • Ek page = ek primary CTA. Baar-baar repeat karo (top, middle, bottom, sticky on mobile).
    • Button contrast mein dikhe; pass mein risk-reducer text: "No spam · 2 minute ka form · COD available".

    Social Proof

    • Star rating + review count headline ke paas.
    • Real photos/videos of customers (stock photos trust kam karti hain).
    • Specific numbers: "2,137 orders" > "thousands of orders".
    • Local proof: "Arera Colony, Kolar, MP Nagar mein 300+ installations".
    • Logos (clients, media, certifications), awards, years in business.

    Page Speed

    Google ke Core Web Vitals targets:

    MetricKya measure karta haiGood
    LCP (Largest Contentful Paint)Main content kitni der mein dikha≤ 2.5s
    INP (Interaction to Next Paint)Click/tap ka response kitna fast≤ 200ms
    CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)Page load hote waqt cheezein kitna hilti hain≤ 0.1

    Check karo: PageSpeed Insights (mobile score), GA4 page load data. Common fixes: images ko WebP/AVIF mein compress, lazy-load below-fold images, kam plugins, fast hosting + CDN, heavy sliders/popups hatao, unused scripts hatao, fonts kam.

    Form Length

    • Har extra field conversions kam karti hai — par quality badha sakti hai. Balance chahiye.
    • Minimum: naam + phone (India mein phone > email). Baaki baad mein call pe.
    • High-ticket mein qualifying fields (budget, timeline) rakhna theek hai.
    • Multi-step form: pehle aasaan sawaal (aapko kya chahiye?), phir contact details. Commitment principle se completion badhti hai.
    • Phone field mein input type "tel", auto +91, inline validation.

    Checkout Experience (e-commerce)

    • Guest checkout — account banane pe force mat karo.
    • Payment options: UPI (sabse popular), cards, wallets, net banking, COD, BNPL/EMI.
    • COD strategy: COD conversions badhata hai par RTO (return to origin) bhi. Tools: COD pe small fee, prepaid pe extra discount, OTP/WhatsApp confirmation of COD orders, pin-code wise COD.
    • One-click / fast checkout solutions (address auto-fill via phone number) India mein checkout drop-off kaafi kam karte hain.
    • Trust badges payment section ke paas, return policy link.
    • Abandoned cart recovery: WhatsApp + SMS + email reminders (1 ghanta, 24 ghante, 72 ghante).

    5.11A/B Testing

    1. Research se problem pakdo: GA4 funnel exploration (kahan drop ho rahe), heatmaps aur session recordings (Microsoft Clarity — free), user surveys.
    2. Hypothesis: "Agar hum price ke saath per-day cost dikhayein, to price objection kam hoga aur CVR badhega."
    3. Prioritize: ICE score — Impact, Confidence, Ease (1-10 each).
    4. Test setup: 50/50 traffic split. Tools: VWO, Convert, Shopify apps, ya Meta/Google ke experiments (alag landing pages ko alag ads se).
    5. Sample size: Har version pe kaafi conversions (rough minimum ~100 per variant achhe decision ke liye; kam traffic mein sirf bade changes test karo).
    6. Duration: Kam se kam 1-2 full weeks (weekday/weekend behaviour alag hota hai). Beech mein winner declare mat karo.
    7. Statistical significance ~95% confidence. Online calculators use karo.
    8. Document: jeeta ho ya haara, learning likho.
    CRO galtiyan
    • Button ka colour test karna jab headline hi confusing hai. Pehle bade levers.
    • 2 din, 15 conversions pe winner declare karna.
    • Ek saath 5 cheezein badalna aur phir pata na chalna kya kaam kiya (ye A/B test nahi, redesign hai — wo bhi theek hai, bas naam sahi do).
    • Desktop pe test karna jabki 80% traffic mobile hai.

    6.6AI ke risks aur rules

    Kabhi mat karo
    • AI ke numbers/facts bina check kiye client ko bhejna. AI confidently galat bol sakta hai (hallucination), especially platform features aur statistics pe.
    • Client ka sensitive data (customer phone numbers, revenue) bina permission public AI tools mein daalna. Company policy aur DPDP ka dhyan rakho.
    • AI-generated "real customer" testimonials ya fake reviews. Ye unethical hai, illegal ho sakta hai, aur platform ban karwa sakta hai.
    • Real logon ke face/voice ka deepfake bina consent.
    • AI se banayi har image ko bina dekhe chala dena — hands, text, product details mein galtiyan hoti hain.
    Disclosure

    Meta aur Google realistic AI-generated/edited content ke liye labels aur policies rakhte hain, khaas karke social issues, politics aur realistic people ke case mein. Apne category ki current policy check karo.

    8.3Interview questions (practice karo)

    20 common interview questions with answer hints
    1. ROAS aur MER mein farak? ROAS platform/campaign level; MER total revenue ÷ total marketing spend, business level.
    2. Break-even ROAS kaise nikaloge? 1 ÷ contribution margin; example ke saath batao.
    3. CPA badh gaya hai. Kaise diagnose karoge? CPM → CTR → CVR breakdown, frequency, tracking check, seasonality, creative fatigue, landing page changes.
    4. Andromeda ne Meta ads kaise badle? Creative-led targeting, consolidation, broad audiences, creative diversity, signal quality.
    5. Advantage+ Sales campaign kab nahi chalaoge? Bahut kam conversion data, strict control/testing chahiye, niche B2B, ya new account jahan signal nahi.
    6. Learning phase kya hai aur kya reset karta hai?
    7. Pixel aur CAPI ka farak, deduplication kaise?
    8. Match types explain karo aur kab broad use karoge?
    9. AI Max for Search kya hai? DSA ka kya hoga?
    10. Quality Score kaise improve karoge?
    11. Target CPA kab use karna chahiye?
    12. GA4 aur Meta ke numbers match kyun nahi karte?
    13. Ek lead gen client ki lead quality kharab hai. 5 steps?
    14. Creative testing ka process batao.
    15. Ek ad ka hook rate high, CTR low. Kya matlab?
    16. ₹1 lakh budget naye D2C brand ke liye kaise baantoge?
    17. UTM naming convention kya rakhoge?
    18. Consent Mode v2 kya hai?
    19. Apni best case study 3 minute mein explain karo.
    20. Ek galti batao jo tumne campaign mein ki aur kya seekha?
    Practical round ke liye ready raho

    Bahut companies assignment deti hain: "Ye account data hai, analysis karo" ya "Is product ke liye 30-din ka Meta + Google plan banao, budget ₹2 lakh." Module 1 ka diagnosis framework, Module 2-3 ka structure, Module 5 ka creative matrix — ye sab yahan kaam aayega. Answer hamesha business numbers se shuru karo (margin, break-even), phir channels.

    8.4Freelancing / agency route

    • Niche chuno: "Coaching institutes ke liye lead gen" > "sab ke liye digital marketing". Niche mein case studies reuse hoti hain aur price zyada milta hai.
    • Pricing models: Fixed monthly retainer, % of ad spend (aam taur pe bade accounts), ya retainer + performance bonus. Shuru mein fixed retainer simple hai.
    • Scope likhit mein: Platforms, number of creatives, reporting frequency, kya included nahi (website development, creative shoots). Ad budget hamesha client ke card se, aapke se nahi.
    • Onboarding checklist: Access (Business Portfolio partner, Google Ads MCC link, GA4, GTM, website admin), unit economics, past performance data, brand assets, goals in numbers.
    • Expectation setting: Pehle 2-4 hafte testing phase. Guarantee kabhi mat do ("100 leads guaranteed"); process aur transparency ki guarantee do.

    8.6Glossary (quick reference)

    AOV
    Average Order Value: revenue ÷ orders
    Andromeda
    Meta ka AI ad-retrieval system jo creative ke basis pe ads ko logon se match karta hai
    Advantage+
    Meta ke automation features aur campaign types (Sales, App, Leads)
    AI Max
    Google Search campaigns ke liye AI feature suite: search term matching, text customization, final URL expansion
    ABO / CBO
    Ad set budget / campaign budget (Advantage campaign budget)
    Attribution
    Conversion ka credit kis touchpoint ko milega, uska rule
    CAC
    Customer Acquisition Cost: total acquisition cost ÷ naye customers
    CAPI
    Meta Conversions API: server se events bhejna
    CMP
    Consent Management Platform: cookie banner tool
    Contribution margin
    Revenue minus COGS aur saare variable costs, % mein
    CPA / CPL
    Cost per acquisition / cost per lead
    CPC / CPM
    Cost per click / cost per 1,000 impressions
    CRO
    Conversion Rate Optimization
    CTR
    Click-through rate: clicks ÷ impressions
    CVR
    Conversion rate: conversions ÷ clicks (ya sessions)
    Data layer
    Website ka structured data jo GTM padhta hai
    Deduplication
    Pixel aur CAPI ke same event ko ek count karna (event_id se)
    DPDP Act
    India ka Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
    EMQ
    Event Match Quality: Meta events ko users se match karne ka score
    Enhanced conversions
    Google ko hashed first-party data bhej ke conversion match improve karna
    Frequency
    Impressions ÷ reach
    gclid / fbclid
    Google / Facebook click IDs jo URL mein aate hain
    Hook rate
    3-second video views ÷ impressions
    Incrementality
    Wo extra result jo sirf ad ki wajah se aaya
    Key event
    GA4 mein important conversion action (pehle "conversion")
    Learning phase
    Naye/edited ad set ka unstable period jab algorithm seekh raha hai
    LTV
    Lifetime Value: customer se poore relationship mein contribution
    MER
    Marketing Efficiency Ratio: total revenue ÷ total marketing spend
    MMM
    Marketing Mix Modeling: statistical model of spend vs sales
    Payback period
    CAC recover hone mein lagne wala time
    PMax
    Performance Max: Google ka all-channel AI campaign
    Quality Score
    Google ka 1-10 keyword quality rating
    ROAS
    Return on Ad Spend: ad revenue ÷ ad spend
    RSA
    Responsive Search Ad: 15 headlines + 4 descriptions
    RTO
    Return to Origin: COD order deliver nahi hua, wapas aaya
    sGTM
    Server-side Google Tag Manager
    TOFU / MOFU / BOFU
    Top / middle / bottom of funnel
    UGC
    User Generated Content: real-customer style content
    UTM
    URL tags jo traffic ka source batate hain
